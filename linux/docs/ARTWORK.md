# Included Tux radio pictures

## Holidays gallery (1.13.3)

The separate **Holidays** tab contains Tux radio pictures for all 11 annual,
nationwide U.S. federal holidays, ordered through the year:

- New Year's Day
- Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. (gallery: Martin Luther King Jr. Day)
- Washington's Birthday
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth National Independence Day (gallery: Juneteenth)
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Columbus Day
- Veterans Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day

Easter and Halloween remain as two additional seasonal cards. The federal set
was checked against the [OPM federal holiday list](https://www.opm.gov/frequently-asked-questions/pay-and-leave-faq/pay-administration/what-are-federal-holidays)
on 2026-11-23. This collection covers the 11 annual nationwide holidays;
location-specific Inauguration Day is not part of that list.

Click a picture, add your callsign or greeting, and choose any SSTV mode.
All 13 holiday PNGs retain their original 1402 × 1122 pixels and are embedded
unchanged. `resources/pictures/holidays/catalog.json` records titles, federal
holiday names, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes. The new seven images and the
existing six were created for Eric Richards using OpenAI's built-in image
generation. Thumbnail previews do not limit the transmit image resolution.
The separate federal holiday image pack contains the full federal set plus
320 × 256 Scottie 2 copies and generation prompts.

## Tux collection (1.13.1)

Tux-sstv 1.13.1 includes 14 full-size Tux amateur-radio cartoons in the
**Tux pictures** gallery below the TX/RX image area. Click a picture or activate
it with Enter, add your callsign or text, and choose any SSTV mode. The gallery
is available locally and on a remote laptop without another download.

The program retains the original 1402 × 1122 PNG when selecting a picture.
Switching modes fits that original to the chosen mode's dimensions; it does
not reuse a Scottie 2 thumbnail or a previously resized TX image. Gallery
thumbnails are only previews. The PNG files are embedded unchanged, so the
collection is available from both launch commands and every installer.

The cartoons are CQ Radio, Mountain Radio, Space Station, Lighthouse,
Retro Radio Shack, Forest Field Day, Rover Adventure, Aurora Radio,
Pirate Radio, Steam Train, Balloon Adventure, Waterfall Expedition,
Rainy Radio Night, and Radio Workshop. Each has space for the operator's
callsign. CQ Radio also has its original CQ lettering.

These raster illustrations were created for Eric Richards (KI5BZO) using
OpenAI's built-in image generation during Tux-sstv development. The release
uses the full-size originals supplied in his Pictures collection, not received
SSTV images, selfies, contact records, or screenshots. The unrelated mystery-dog
cartoon is not included in this Tux collection.

`resources/pictures/tux/catalog.json` records each title, filename, original
dimensions and SHA-256 digest. The adjacent PNGs are the source artwork;
`resources/tux_pictures.qrc` embeds them. This provenance note does not select
a license for the application or artwork.

Your own **Transmit pictures** list and **Receive history** stay separate.
Removing a personal photo does not remove the built-in Tux collection or
delete any image file.
